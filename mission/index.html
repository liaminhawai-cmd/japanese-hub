ミッション：日本MISSION: JAPAN · MISSION 1: THE RAMEN RECIPE

An Aussie ramen chain wants the recipe from a one-star shop in Tokyo. You are the agent. There is no set order: land, wander, eat, get papers, make friends, make enemies, find out when the place opens, get inside, get the recipe out, call it in. Everyone you meet has their own problems and most of them are more interesting than yours.

Proof of concept. One mission, seven places, eleven people, one crow. Every Japanese line is a draft for a teacher to check. Nothing is locked and nothing is on rails. Leaving through the menu saves; leaving any other way is you zoning out in public.

Field manual

Talk to anyone about anything they have going on. Look reads the place, and signs are in Japanese. Use an item on the place or on a person; some combinations are wise, some are very funny, one gets you deported. Go moves you and takes an hour. Energy drops as you move; food fixes it, sleep at the hotel fixes everything and starts a new day. Suspicion only matters where people are watching you.
