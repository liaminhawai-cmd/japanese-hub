Read a story, open the notebook when you need it, then show what you understood

Proof of concept for review. Two original retellings of traditional folk tales at two levels. The Japanese is a draft for a teacher to check, sentence by sentence, before any class reads it.

Pick a story. Read it with the furigana on or off. The notebook has the key words; open it only when you need it. Then three short rounds: quick read, find the evidence, and your own ideas.

Tap a phrase with a line under it to see the grammar that powers it. Tap a notebook word to light it up in the story.

right first try on quick read and find the evidence. Missed ones came back until you got them.
