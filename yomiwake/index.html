One kanji, more than one reading

Proof of concept for review — built from a Japanese teacher's request: exercises on the furigana a kanji takes in different words (兄 vs お兄さん). The kanji set here is a draft seed, not agreed content.

A kanji does not carry one fixed sound. The word around it decides how it is read. Pick a kanji and meet its readings.

Tap a word: furigana first, then meaning. It fades back to plain kanji by itself.
